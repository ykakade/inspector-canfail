# Reviewer illustration

Tool: built-in image generation tool.

Output: `reviewer.png`.

Format: PNG with a transparent background and opaque white shapes inside the portrait.

The image is an original character. Ponytail is the reference for the compact black-and-white portrait format.

## Initial prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small open-source developer-tool README mascot, an original black-and-white editorial ink portrait, intended to display at 200 pixels wide on GitHub. Subject: a skeptical experienced software test reviewer, a woman in her 50s, short tousled dark hair, narrow rectangular reading glasses low on her nose, looking directly at the viewer with one slightly raised eyebrow, calm and understated. She holds one small plain sheet at chest height with a single simple X mark on it, the cue for a test that can fail. Crop: head, shoulders, and hand holding the sheet, centered as a compact cutout. Style: bold imperfect black brush-and-pen contours, economical linework, large clean white shapes, a few rough ink marks, early open-source hacker illustration or photocopied engineering zine. Strong silhouette, readable at small size. Two colors only: black ink and white face/paper shapes. Transparent background outside the silhouette; white areas inside face and paper stay opaque. The overall presence should be like a quiet code reviewer who asks whether a passing test can detect an error. No ponytail, no long hair, no beard. No text, no letters, no speech bubble, no UI, no computer, no circuit-board patterns, no robot, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no polished corporate vector style, no photorealism, no decorative frame. Original character, not a copy of any existing mascot.

## Simplification prompt

Edit the reviewer illustration from the preceding image. Preserve the original character: older woman, short dark hair, rectangular glasses, skeptical raised eyebrow, paper with one X. Simplify the drawing drastically into a compact bold black-ink caricature for a 200px GitHub README logo. Aim for the economy and roughness of an old hacker-project mascot: large flat black and white shapes, only a few thick uneven contours. Remove the realistic rendering, eyelashes, fine wrinkles, all fine hair strands, all crosshatching, fabric seams, texture shading, and almost all torso. Keep the face, glasses, one small hand and X paper close to the cheek. The head should dominate 75% of the image. Crop into a small, tight bust without a large blank margin. Expression: dry and quietly skeptical, closed mouth without a smile. Only black and opaque white within the silhouette, genuine transparent background outside. No text, gradients, shadows, frame, UI, robot, ponytail, or photographic detail. Flat, handmade, dead simple, legible at 200px.
